## 🏛️ Core Features

- **🔐 Student Authentication:** Login using **College Roll Number** and **10-Digit Registered Mobile Number** with department personalization.
- **📅 Academic Time Table:** Real-time period tracking, batch B1/B2 split for practicals, morning tea recess, and canteen lunch break.
- **📚 Notes & Labs Vault:** Handwritten topper notes, solved 5-year PYQs, and practical lab manuals with **External Examiner Viva Voce Q&A**.
- **⚡ DCE Campus Radar:** Live occupancy and silence tracker across the Central Library and Aryabhata Labs.
- **🍛 Canteen & Nescafe:** Live daily food menus with authentic INR (₹) student pricing.
- **🗺️ Campus Navigator:** Pinpoint Sharma Xerox, late-night Maggi points, and RO water coolers.
- **👥 Peer Study Circles:** Cram groups for GATE CSE 2027, Striver SDE sheet, and End-Sem exams.
- **🛡️ Suraksha SOS:** Zero tolerance anti-ragging helpline (1800-180-5522) and night security escorts.
- **🏛️ About Us & DCE Legacy:** AICTE approved, NAAC accredited, 26+ years of pedagogy, campus contacts, and directory.
